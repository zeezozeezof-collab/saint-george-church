Our Church

A living piece of Jordanian Christian heritage

Saint George Church, also known locally as Al-Khader Church, stands in the historic Al-Khader area of Al-Salt. The church belongs to the Greek Orthodox tradition and remains both a place of worship and an important heritage destination.

☦

Faith beneath historic stone

The church was built in 1682 over an ancient cave during the Ottoman period. Its stone architecture, icons, wooden details and quiet atmosphere connect today's worshippers with centuries of local history.


🕯

A house of prayer

Visitors encounter a peaceful sacred space filled with icons, candles and traditional Orthodox furnishings. The church is also remembered as a place where people come to pray and seek blessing.

The Story of the Church

A sacred story carved into the stone of Al-Salt

Saint George Church, also known as Al-Khader Church, is one of the best-known Christian heritage sites in historic Al-Salt. The church rises above an ancient cave and forms part of the city's remarkable landscape of old stone buildings, churches and mosques.

🕯

The sacred cave

The sanctuary was built over a cave and is closely connected with local traditions surrounding Saint George. The cave remains one of the most distinctive parts of the pilgrimage experience.

🐎

Saint George

Saint George is traditionally depicted as a Christian knight on horseback defeating a dragon. This image is especially important to the visual identity of the church and its icons.

🏛

Al-Salt heritage

The church sits within the historic heart of Al-Salt, a city famous for its golden limestone architecture and its tradition of religious coexistence.

Heritage & Story

More than a building

1682

Historic foundation

Official Jordanian heritage and tourism sources date the Al-Khader / Saint George shrine to the 17th century, with the church built in 1682.

🕊

Peace & coexistence

Jordan's tourism story highlights the church as a place visited by Christians and Muslims alike, reflecting the historic spirit of coexistence in Al-Salt.

⛪

Living Orthodox tradition

The church continues to serve a worshipping community, with liturgical life, clergy and parish groups forming part of its ongoing story.
